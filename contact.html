FAQ

Common questions

I've never trained. Where do I start?

Adult Fundamentals (Monday and Wednesday at 6:00 PM) is built for new students. The midday All Levels class is also beginner-friendly.

Do I need to be in shape first?

No. Jiu-jitsu is how a lot of our students got in shape. You'll train at your own pace, and you can sit out any round.

What should I wear?

Comfortable athletic clothes without zippers, buttons or pockets, like a rash guard or T-shirt and athletic shorts. If you have a gi, bring it. Bring water and flip-flops for walking off the mat.

What is open mat?

Open mat is unstructured training time. There's no class; students use it to roll, drill techniques and work on their game.
