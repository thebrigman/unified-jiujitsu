Your first class

Getting started is simple

No experience or fitness level required. Everyone on the mat started exactly where you are.

  1. Book a free trial

    Fill out the short form and we'll get back to you to confirm a class that fits your schedule.

  2. Arrive 15 minutes early

    Wear comfortable athletic clothes without zippers or pockets, and bring water. If you have a gi, bring it.

  3. Train at your pace

    Your instructor will walk you through the techniques and pair you with a partner. Go at whatever pace feels right.
